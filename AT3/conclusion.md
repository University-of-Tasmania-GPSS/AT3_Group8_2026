This investigation demonstrated how GIS-based multi-criteria modelling can be used to assess 
relative susceptibility to coastal washout and ground collapse at Long Beach. 
The integration of independently produced coastal vulnerability datasets showed that modelling 
methodology can substantially influence susceptibility classifications. The fuzzy-informed model 
classified a greater proportion of the coastal corridor as High or Very High susceptibility than the 
conventional CVI-informed model. 
Separately, agreement among the original six models identified 21 priority investigation zones. 
Although these results do not confirm the presence of subsurface voids, they provide a systematic 
approach for identifying coastal areas that may benefit from targeted field investigations. 
Overall, the study demonstrates the potential of integrating terrain, drainage, coastal vulnerability 
and material characteristics to support coastal asset assessment and investigation planning.
